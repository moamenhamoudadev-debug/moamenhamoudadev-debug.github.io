infoOVERVIEW / PRODUCT PROFILE

About UniNest

UniNest helps out-of-town university students find suitable housing near campus (Fayoum, Cairo, Alexandria and more). Students set their interests and the site suggests a housing partner who shares them, so living together goes smoothly. Property owners can create an account and upload their apartments, and the system prevents a male and a female from booking the same housing unit.

warning SECTION 01 / THE CHALLENGE

The Problem

Students who move to another city for university struggle to find housing that is affordable, close to campus and a good fit — and often end up sharing with strangers they don't get along with.

  • cancel Scattered Listings: Housing offers are spread across social media groups and word of mouth, hard to compare and hard to trust.
  • cancel Incompatible Flatmates: Students are placed with strangers who have different habits and interests.
  • cancel No Booking Rules: Nothing stops a male and a female from ending up booked into the same housing unit.
verified SECTION 02 / THE ARCHITECTURAL ANSWER

The Solution

A single platform where students search and filter listings, set their interests to find a compatible housing partner, and owners create accounts to publish their apartments.

  • check_circle Interest-Based Matching: Students list their interests and get matched with flatmates who share them.
  • check_circle Gender-Aware Booking: The system blocks mixed-gender bookings of the same housing unit.
  • check_circle Owner Accounts: Owners upload and manage their apartments from their own profile.
